# Gentle Hands Home Care website

## Purpose and audience

Help families understand home-care options and prepare a practical care conversation. Visitors may be arranging support for an older relative or planning a return home after hospital care. The interface should feel calm, readable and direct, including on a small phone.

## Primary journey

Understand the service → explore a care category → understand how care is planned → prepare the next conversation. The care note and question guide support that journey as secondary tools.

## Scope

The approved homepage now connects to About, a service directory, five service detail pages, a guide directory, three care-planning articles, FAQs, Contact and factual website privacy information. Service categories are preview content to be confirmed by the business. No booking, payments, clinical assessment, accounts, enquiry submission or personal-data collection is implemented. Careers, partnership programmes and membership products are not part of the confirmed business scope.

## Confirm before public launch

The business must supply phone/email, service area, confirmed service list, professional qualifications where relevant, availability and policies. Do not invent credentials, testimonials, response guarantees, prices, coverage areas or care outcomes. Generated imagery must not be represented as a real patient or team photograph.

## Brand input

Use the supplied Gentle Hands logo and its navy, turquoise and gold. Keep the full original seal available. Copy should respect dignity, independence and family involvement without overstating medical capabilities.
