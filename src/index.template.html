Questions families ask.

A few practical details to help you prepare.

How do I know which care service to explore?

Start with daily needs and the treating clinician’s recommendations. Nursing, physiotherapy and everyday assistance have different roles. A clinical assessment may be needed to determine the appropriate support.

Can I plan support before hospital discharge?

Planning ahead allows time to discuss discharge advice, the home environment, equipment if needed, and service availability. Confirm the plan with the treating team before making arrangements.

What should be included in a care quote?

Ask about the service, visit or shift length, tasks included, caregiver role and any additional charges. Confirm payment and cancellation terms before arranging care.

Where is care available?

The service area and local availability need to be confirmed directly with Gentle Hands before arranging care. Contact details are still being finalised for this homepage preview.
