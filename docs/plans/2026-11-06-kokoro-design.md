# Kokoro care redesign

Build a complete responsive reinterpretation of https://kokoro.plus/home/ with retained brand and service scope. Use warm ivory, forest green, generous editorial typography, original concise copy, and locally bundled reference imagery. Prioritize finding appropriate support and preparing an enquiry. Avoid unsupported clinical, cost, deployment, or testimonial claims.

Use dependency-free semantic HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Include service filtering and detail dialogs, a three-step care finder, a validated enquiry form with explicit WhatsApp handoff, equipment detail controls, FAQ disclosures, responsive navigation, and reduced-motion support. Do not simulate a completed booking or callback submission. Publish as a private Site.
